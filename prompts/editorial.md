# Editorial rules

- Lead with the most consequential *verified* change. If nothing verified changed, say
  "No new verified, material change" and stop; do not pad with repeats or old news.
- Every factual sentence carries a link and a status label (Verified / Corroborated /
  Single source / Interested-party claim / Unverified / Contradicted).
- Headings are short and literal. Tables for anything with more than three comparable items.
- Numbers: Indian units (lakh, crore), ₹/sq ft with basis stated ("land", "super built-up").
  Never place land rates and saleable rates in the same column without labels.
- Forecasts are scenarios: "base case 7%/yr (assumes …)", never "will grow 7%".
- Interested parties are named as such: "the developer says", "a broker video claims".
- Uncertainty is visible: "Not verified", "Insufficient evidence to estimate", "Unranked".
- "Research failed" ≠ "No new updates". Use the run status exactly.
- Infographic: one headline (the lead verified item), ≤2 KPI tiles from usable claims,
  generated illustrations only, source line at the bottom.
