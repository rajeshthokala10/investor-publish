From the editor

The latest quarter brought a firmer market. According to ANAROCK, Bengaluru housing sales rose 12% YoY to 16,670 units in Q3 2026. The agency also says MMR and Bengaluru together accounted for 48% of total sales across the seven cities it tracks, a reminder of how much national demand now rests on these two markets.

Jobs continue to underpin that demand. Colliers says Bengaluru led Jan-Sep 2026 office leasing at 15.7 million sq ft, driven partly by GCCs and flex operators. ANAROCK adds that GCCs made up 70% of the roughly 10.8 million sq ft of office absorption in H1 2026. Where offices cluster, housing follows.

Infrastructure is the other half of the story, and timelines deserve a careful read. BMRCL's Blue Line has reached 72% overall construction progress, per Business Today, yet Construction World reports the full opening has slipped to March 2028. K-RIDE says it is committed to starting suburban rail operations by December 2028. Buy for today's connectivity, not tomorrow's.

ANAROCK data show Bengaluru housing sales rose 12% YoY to 16,670 units in Q3 2026, from 14,835 units a year earlier. Knight Frank India puts Jan-Sep 2026 sales at 43,140 units, up 5% YoY and second only to Mumbai.

Prices continue to move up. ANAROCK reports an annual rise of 8% in Q3 2026, and says average prices went from Rs 4,975 to Rs 9,450 per sq ft between 2019 and Q2 2026, while gross rental yield improved from 3.6% to 4.6%.

Supply is the counterweight. ANAROCK says unsold stock rose 34% YoY in Q2 2026, the fastest among the top seven cities, while launches rose 41% YoY to around 21,670 units. Buyers have choice and, in many projects, room to negotiate.

EAST BENGALURU

East: Metro Hopes, Big Launches

Whitefield, Sarjapur Road and KR Puram are where JLL sees growth shifting, with metro lines and large launches shaping the next phase.

JLL's Q2 2026 report says Bengaluru prices rose 3.1% and rents 2.5% quarter-on-quarter, with growth shifting to Whitefield, Sarjapur Road and Yelahanka. The same report notes new supply was concentrated along Bellary Road, followed by Hosur Road and Whitefield. Swarajya reports Whitefield land values rose 23 per cent, though the period it covers is not fully stated.

Metro is the east's main catalyst. Business Today reports the Blue Line, which passes KR Pura, has reached 72% overall progress, with remaining construction aimed for June 2027 before phased commissioning. Trial runs between KR Pura and HSR Layout were expected by October 2026. The Sarjapur–Hebbal Red Line is a proposal: the cabinet cleared its DPR, but central approval is pending, with completion proposed for April 2033.

Developers are committing capital. Brigade Enterprises announced Brigade Belvedere on Budigere Road, a 10.75-acre development with 773 units in the initial phase. Deccan Herald reported that Embassy signed a JDA for a 17.9-acre Whitefield parcel with a projected GDV of ₹1,600 crore. Further out, Hoskote benefits from the 80 km Dobaspet–Hoskote STRR stretch, which has been operational for over two years.

NORTH BENGALURU

North: Airport Belt Gathers Pace

Hebbal, Yelahanka and Devanahalli are drawing launches, with metro, suburban rail and KWIN City all promised, but timelines have slipped.

NewsFirst Prime puts Hebbal apartments at ₹9,500–12,500 per sq ft on average and Yelahanka apartments at ₹7,500–9,500 per sq ft. For Devanahalli it cites plots at ₹6,000–10,000 per sq ft and premium villas above ₹10,000 per sq ft, with prices rising 15–20% annually. These are media estimates of asking levels, so compare against live listings before committing.

Connectivity is the case for the north, and the delays are the caution. The Blue Line's Phase 2B runs from KR Pura to the airport via Hebbal and Yelahanka (38.44 km), yet Construction World reports the full opening has moved to March 2028. Railway Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw said in May 2026 that suburban rail construction was progressing on all four corridors. Karnataka Cabinet cleared a Rs 2,215 crore Hebbal–Mekhri Circle project.

Employment and launches add depth. South First reports Toyota Kirloskar will invest Rs 1,200 crore over five years in KWIN City, with an MoU signed. Embassy Developments launched Embassy Origins, an 85-acre project, on 16 Sept 2026 and reports 62% absorption of launched units within a week. Century Real Estate says The Astoria at Jakkur has crossed ₹550 crore in bookings.

SOUTH BENGALURU

South: Yellow Line and Pink Line

Electronic City, Bommasandra and the Hosur Road belt rely on metro access, while Attibele, Harohalli and Hosur depend on proposals.

JLL's October 2025 insight cited residential prices of INR 7,000-7,500 per sq ft near Electronic City station and INR 6,000-6,500 near Bommasandra. It anticipated appreciation of 10-15% within the following 6-12 months near Yellow Line stations, a forecast rather than an outcome. Hosur Road is also among the areas where JLL saw new supply concentrated in Q2 2026.

The Pink Line is the nearest change. ETV Bharat reports the 7.5-km elevated stretch from Kalena Agrahara to Tavarekere is ready and will open once the Union government confirms an inauguration date. It has six stations, including Hulimavu, IIMB and an interchange at Jayadeva Hospital. A 15 August commissioning target was missed, and a safety inspection was set for 6–8 October.

Beyond that, plans are early. BMRCL feasibility work covers an 11-km Bommasandra–Attibele extension, and a DPR was sought for a Jigani extension of another 11-12 km. Tamil Nadu is pursuing a 23-km Hosur–Bommasandra metro, though BMRCL has said a direct connection is not technically feasible. Harohalli is under consideration for a second airport on about 9,000 acres, subject to feasibility.

WEST BENGALURU

West: Bidadi and Warehouse Corridors

The west mixes established Tumkur Road and Rajajinagar markets with a contested Bidadi township and a booming Nelamangala warehouse belt.

The Bidadi township is the west's biggest question. South First reports final notifications for 5,462 acres across seven villages, with the Greater Bengaluru Development Authority as developer, and land priced at ₹2.30 crore an acre for first payments. Swarajya reports apartment prices in Bidadi fell close to 15 per cent last year. The state has formed a review committee, and forty farmer organisations are preparing to move court.

Nelamangala is the industrial story. Cushman & Wakefield data show Bengaluru warehouse leasing up 78% y/y to 3.1 MSF in H1 2026, with vacancy falling to 11% from 13%, and Nelamangala and Dabaspete leading activity. The western corridor accounted for 52% of leasing, and warehouse rentals are rising 4-5% annually.

In established pockets, Brigade Group puts Tumkur Road apartments at ₹5,500–8,500 per sq ft and Rajajinagar at ₹9,000–14,000. It cites gross yields of 4–5% for Yeshwanthpur against 3–4% for Tumkur Road, and notes Nagasandra station gives Tumkur Road operational metro access. Phase 3's Hosahalli–Kadabagere corridor along Magadi Road spans 12.5 km.

BUYER’S GUIDE

Plots, villas or apartments?

Villa plots

Plotted layouts are selling in the north. KNS Candrill in Doddaballapur reports selling all 233 Phase 1 plots within six months, for over Rs 100 crore. NewsFirst Prime puts Devanahalli plots at ₹6,000–10,000 per sq ft. Plots carry the highest approval risk: BMRDA frames rules for local planning authorities but has little role in layout approval, so verify authority sanction before paying.

Villas

Villas are concentrated in the north. Embassy Riverine, part of Embassy Origins Phase 1, offers 217 villas in 4, 4.5 and 5 BHK formats across roughly 1.1 million sq ft. NewsFirst Prime says premium villas in Devanahalli sell above ₹10,000 per sq ft.

Apartments

Apartments dominate launches. Embassy South Reserve offers 855 apartments, studio to 3.5 BHK, and both Phase 1 projects are described as RERA-registered. ANAROCK puts Bengaluru gross rental yield at 4.6%, while Sobha's blog puts Hebbal at about 2%. Yeshwanthpur's 4–5% gross yield, per Brigade Group, suggests rental returns vary widely by micro-market.

Apartments near operating or near-complete metro lines offer the clearest value. Plots and villas on the periphery suit patient buyers who can verify approvals and absorb delays. With unsold stock rising, take time to compare projects.

OUTLOOK

What to expect next

Demand looks steady rather than frenzied. ANAROCK reports Bengaluru sales growth of 12% YoY in Q3 2026, with an annual price rise of 8%. Knight Frank India records 5% growth for Jan-Sep 2026. Office leasing, led by GCCs, continues to support housing demand in the east and north.

Infrastructure will set the pace of micro-market gains. Business Today reports the Pink Line's underground stretch is targeted for March 2027 and the Blue Line's remaining construction for June 2027, while Construction World reports a full Blue Line opening in March 2028. Suburban rail operations are committed for December 2028. Treat all of these as targets.

On the watch list

  • Pink Line elevated opening — The 7.5-km stretch is ready and awaits an inauguration date from the Union government.
  • Bidadi review committee report — The committee headed by Justice Guhanathan Narendar was to report within 30 days.
  • STRR remaining stretch — The remaining 21 km to the border is targeted for completion by November 2026.
  • Red Line central approval — The Sarjapur–Hebbal DPR awaits central approval, with completion proposed for April 2033.
  • Second airport site — Harohalli is one option; no final site chosen and a consultant is studying shortlisted locations.
  • Hebbal–Silk Board tunnel award — Adani Enterprises is lowest bidder; no award has been confirmed.
  • Unsold stock — ANAROCK says it rose 34% YoY in Q2 2026, the fastest among top seven cities.
