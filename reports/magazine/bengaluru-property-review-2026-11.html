From the editor

Demand is holding up. According to ANAROCK, Bengaluru sold about 16,670 homes in Q3 2026, with sales up 12% year on year and average prices up 8%.

Yet inventory is building. ANAROCK reported that unsold stock rose 34% to about 79,180 units in Q2 2026, while launches climbed 41% year on year to about 21,670 units.

Infrastructure will decide micro-market fortunes. BMRCL's managing director targets Blue Line trial runs between Byappanahalli, KR Pura and Marathahalli around 15 Nov 2026, though Swarajya reports the wider train-operations deadline has moved to 2027.

Analysts count differently. PropEquity recorded 21,516 housing sales in Q2 2026, ANAROCK about 15,285, and JLL put residential demand at approximately 17,000 units. All point to a large, active market, but buyers should compare figures from the same source.

Premium homes dominate. Deccan Herald, citing Anarock, reported that homes above Rs 1.5 crore made up 62% of Bengaluru residential sales in H1 2026, and ANAROCK says 96% of Q2 launches were priced above Rs 80 lakh.

Jobs underpin demand. Knight Frank says Bengaluru was India's largest office leasing market in H1 2026, with 14.1 million sq ft of absorption, and JLL notes Whitefield led quarterly office rent growth in Q2 2026.

THE BIG PICTURE

The government’s plan for Bengaluru

Timelines are moving. BMRCL targets Blue Line trial runs in mid-November, while Swarajya reports the full line is now expected to be fully operational by March 2028, against an earlier December 2027 deadline.

Several large plans remain proposals. Phase 4 corridors are not yet finalised or approved, and Phase 3A has Karnataka Cabinet approval but awaits Government of India approval.

EAST BENGALURU

East: Metro Nears, Rains Linger

Whitefield and the ORR belt await the Blue Line, while road works and monsoon flooding test residents' patience.

The Blue Line dominates East Bengaluru. BMRCL's managing director says all ORR works except signalling are complete, with trial runs targeted around 15 Nov 2026. Construction World puts the KR Puram–Central Silk Board stretch at 87% complete, and BMRCL is considering a partial KR Puram–Marathahalli service.

Swarajya, citing an official, says commercial opening is unlikely by end-2026, with key works incomplete at Marathahalli and Central Silk Board. Sobha expects the Silk Board–KR Puram part by end-2026. The Greater Bengaluru Authority plans Rs 450 crore of ORR white-topping from October 2026 to January 2027, a likely traffic disruption.

Prices lean on developer figures. Brigade's blog puts Whitefield at ₹13,000/sq ft in early 2026, with 13% annual appreciation, while Sobha cites around ₹15,000/sq ft for Greater Whitefield. JLL says Whitefield led quarterly office rent growth at 1.3%. After a downpour, rainwater entered apartment complexes near Varthur Road.

NORTH BENGALURU

North: Airport Belt Draws Capital

Hebbal, Yelahanka and Devanahalli attract launches and jobs, but Blue Line timelines have lengthened.

Metro progress is the swing factor. Business Today reported the Blue Line at 72% overall construction progress and described Phase 2B as 38.44 km from KR Pura to the airport via Hebbal and Yelahanka. Swarajya reports the Hebbal–airport section is targeted by September 2027 and the full line by March 2028, against December 2027 earlier.

Demand follows offices and jobs. JLL describes the Hebbal-Devanahalli corridor as the primary hub for new Grade-A office and residential developments, with new supply concentrated along Bellary Road. Business Today reports Foxconn's Devanahalli plant recruited nearly 30,000 staff.

Launches are large. Embassy Developments reports over ₹1,800 crore of pre-sales at Embassy Origins, an 85-acre project launched 16 September 2026. Mahindra Lifespaces expects to launch a 10.05-acre project near the airport within nine months of 27 October 2026.

SOUTH BENGALURU

South: Metro Pending, Launches Strong

Bannerghatta Road, Hosur Road and Kanakapura Road draw buyers while the Pink Line opening and airport siting remain open.

The Pink Line elevated stretch is the headline wait. BMRCL targeted 15 August for commissioning the 7.5-km section between Kalena Agrahara and Tavarekere, and a minister said on 2 Sep 2026 it was ready and awaiting a date from the Union government. Reports found no opening confirmation as of 1 Nov 2026.

Godrej Properties reports selling more than 1,000 homes worth over Rs 2,000 crore at Godrej Vanantara off Bannerghatta Road, launched in the first week of June 2026. Puravankara's blog cites a Hosa Road average of about ₹10,885/sq ft, within a range of ₹6,600–18,285.

Further south, Swarajya reported on 9 July 2026 that two shortlisted sites for a second airport lie near Harohalli on Kanakapura Road, with no final location selected. Sobha lists Kanakapura Road villas at ₹6,200–11,000 per sq ft. The Attibele STRR section is nearly finished and targeted for November 2026.

WEST BENGALURU

West: Bidadi Township Divides Opinion

A proposed township at Bidadi and Tumkur Road logistics anchor the west, while land acquisition remains contested.

The Bidadi Greater Bengaluru Integrated Township is proposed across about 9,600 acres in nine revenue villages, with project cost estimated at Rs 18,133 crore, according to The News Minute. South First reports final acquisition notifications covering 5,462 acres across seven villages.

Compensation is the flashpoint. Deputy CM DK Shivakumar says the state will pay over ₹3 crore per acre, and landowners who opt out of cash can receive 50 percent of developed land. The News Minute reported 40 farmer organisations preparing to move court, and the JD(S) demanded the notifications be withdrawn.

Elsewhere, JLL says Tumkur Road led warehousing demand, with citywide logistics rents at INR 24.7 per sq ft per month, up 4.5% year on year. The Phase 3 Kadabagare–Hosahalli line along Magadi Road is listed as 12.5 km, elevated, with 9 stations; no BMRCL deadline is finalised.

BUYER’S GUIDE

Plots, villas or apartments?

Villa plots

Plots are tied to peripheral plans. Brigade lists Oasis Phase 3 on the STRR at Devanahalli as 987 plots from INR 1.09 Crore onwards, and marks the phase sold out. Puravankara's blog lists Purva Tivoli Hills as 889 plots across about 60 acres. Buyers should check approvals, as BIAAPA found builders selling sites near the airport without mandatory approval.

Villas

Sobha lists villa prices at ₹6,000–11,500 per sq ft in Devanahalli and ₹6,200–11,000 on Kanakapura Road, while Sarjapur Road spans ₹3,212–29,424. The very wide Sarjapur band points to very different products under one label.

Apartments

ANAROCK says 96% of Q2 2026 launches were priced above Rs 80 lakh. Tata Housing's Varnam in Devanahalli has 582 apartments and 48 townhouses, with PTI reporting sales above ₹1,000 crore. Embassy South Reserve has 855 apartments from studio to 3.5 BHK.

Apartments in metro-linked or job-led pockets carry the clearest demand signals. Plots and villas depend more on approvals and on infrastructure that is still proposed. Compare like with like, and favour projects near roads and rails already operating.

OUTLOOK

What to expect next

ANAROCK data show prices up 8% year on year while unsold stock stands at about 79,180 units, so buyers have choice even as prices rise. JLL reports office vacancy rose to 11.4% in Q2 2026.

Infrastructure dates are the main variable: Blue Line trials around 15 Nov 2026, the Revised Master Plan-2041 expected to be implemented by end of 2027, and K-RIDE committed to starting suburban operations by December 2028, according to Asianet Newsable.

On the watch list

  • Blue Line trial runs — BMRCL targets trials between Byappanahalli, KR Pura and Marathahalli around 15 Nov 2026.
  • STRR completion — Swarajya targets November 2026; NHAI targets December 2026 for the girder launch.
  • Pink Line opening — The elevated stretch is reportedly ready, awaiting final approvals and an opening date.
  • Bidadi acquisition — Farmer organisations are preparing to move court; compensation terms remain contested.
  • ORR white-topping — Rs 450 crore of works planned October 2026 to January 2027 may disrupt traffic.
  • Second airport site — A feasibility report is planned; shortlisted sites lie near Harohalli and Nelamangala.
  • Tunnel road litigation — The High Court says any contract remains subject to the outcome of the PILs.
