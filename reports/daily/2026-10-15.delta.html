Pink Line faces safety scrutiny as BMRCL prepares reply

Deccan Herald reports 36 safety and technical deficiencies on the Bannerghatta Road corridor; BMRCL is expected to respond to inspection observations next week.

Deccan Herald reported on 15 October that the Pink Line faces 36 safety and technical deficiencies, a day after the corridor was described as nearing launch following a safety review.

Metro Rail News said BMRCL received observations from the Commissioner of Metro Rail Safety on 9 October and planned to submit its compliance report on 13 October.

The Hindu reported on 13 October that BMRCL would respond to the safety inspection observations next week.

Urban Acres reports the line is targeting a March 2027 launch despite delays.

Growth towns to watch

Hoskote

The 80 km Dobaspet–Hoskote STRR stretch is operational and Karnataka's 71-km expressway section was reported opened; the remaining 21 km of STRR is targeted for November 2026.

Devanahalli

Airport-adjacent market where suburban rail Corridor 1 alignment has been approved by Indian Railways, with joint land measurement under way.

Doddaballapur

KWIN City is a 5,800-acre project, and Toyota Kirloskar plans a Rs 1,200 crore BizIntel Hub there; a plotted project sold 233 plots.

Nelamangala

Warehouse leasing led by Nelamangala and Dabaspete, with the western corridor taking 52% of leasing in H1 2026.

Bidadi

A GBDA-led township with 5,462 acres notified across seven villages; total footprint of 7,481 acres across nine villages is expected.

Harohalli

About 40 km from the city centre, it is under consideration for a second airport on about 9,000 acres, with completion expected 2032-33.
