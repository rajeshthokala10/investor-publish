GBA chief orders junction plan for Bannerghatta Road

After inspecting footpaths, Chief Commissioner M Maheshwar Rao directed officials to prepare a detailed project report for junctions between Gottigere and Tavarekere.

GBA Chief Commissioner M Maheshwar Rao directed officials to prepare a DPR for junctions between Gottigere and Tavarekere on Bannerghatta Road, according to The Bengaluru Live. The direction followed his inspection of the road's footpaths around 13 October. The paper notes it is a direction, not completed works.

The inspection found pedestrians lacked a safe pathway near Kalena Agrahara metro station, The Bengaluru Live reported. Rao ordered missing drain slabs replaced and mid-block median crossings built where needed.

The plan drew wide coverage. The Hindu and Deccan Herald report the GBA will prepare the DPR, NewsFirst Prime reports junction upgrades and safer footpaths are planned, and The New Indian Express reports the junctions are to be developed for smoother traffic movement.

INVESTMENT COLUMN

Reading Bengaluru's growth areas by stage

Bidadi's township and AI city is approved, with the GBDA as executing agency. The BMRDA's proposed integrated townships name Bidadi as pilot, and PropNewz reports industrial township land. Buyers might check approvals, title and water before treating approval as delivery.

Q-City at Hesaraghatta has land sanctioned. SWIFT City, planned across 1,000 acres with 150 ft roads, and KWIN City, over 5,800 acres with a planned monorail, are only announced. Announced projects carry execution risk worth weighing.

Karnataka dropped acquisition at the Devanahalli aerospace park and the plan was reported scrapped. Harohalli is the leading contender for a second airport but remains proposed. The Bengaluru Business Corridor is awarded, while the Dobbaspet logistics hub's stage is unclear.

Near the city, Puravankara's Sathanur deal and the Karanth Layout show interest is following concrete steps. Even so, the Hebbal tunnel road's flagged concerns are a reminder to check funding before relying on a driver. These are considerations, not advice.
