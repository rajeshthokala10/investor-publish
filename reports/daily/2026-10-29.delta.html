Pink Line elevated stretch nears opening after safety nod

Minister says CMRS clearance is in hand for the 7.5-km Kalena Agrahara–Tavarekere section; inauguration is tied to a possible mid-November visit by the Prime Minister.

Karnataka minister Krishna Byre Gowda said on 28 October that the 7.5-km elevated Pink Line between Kalena Agrahara and Tavarekere has received CMRS safety clearance, according to The Bengaluru Live.

The report says the inauguration is planned around Prime Minister Narendra Modi's proposed 15 November visit to Bengaluru. Passenger service depends on separate authorisation, and no start date has been confirmed.

The Hindu reported that the Chief Minister said the corridor is likely to be inaugurated on 15 November. Deccan Herald reported that Mr Modi is likely to inaugurate the line next month, and also that it may open on 3 or 15 November, dates tied to possible Prime Ministerial visits.

The Times of India reports the 7.5km Pink Line will take Bengaluru's Metro network beyond 100km.

COURTS

High Court lets GBA seal illegal PGs

On 27 October the Karnataka High Court, Justice Suraj Govindaraj presiding, allowed the Greater Bengaluru Authority to seal illegally operating PG accommodations after repeated non-compliance, reports Oneindia.

A GBA audit of 5,480 PGs found 3,597 operating without licences, according to the report.

The Hindu reports the GBA is planning stricter safety norms for PG accommodations. Deccan Herald and The Times of India carried the High Court's go-ahead for action against erring PG centres.

INVESTMENT COLUMN

Reading Bengaluru's outer growth areas carefully

Several outer areas rest on announcements rather than construction. SWIFT City in Sarjapur has 1,000 acres earmarked and is at the announced stage; KWIN City, spread over 5,800 acres near Doddaballapura, is also announced. Buyers may wish to check what has been notified.

Bidadi's GBIT / AI City is described as approved, while Hesaraghatta's Quantum City is announced with a 2035 target. A second airport at Harohalli is only proposed. Stage matters more than headlines.

Roads are further along: the Bengaluru Business Corridor (PRR) is at the tendered stage. Closer in, the Pink Line's expected opening shows what execution looks like.

Considerations, not advice: check approvals, clear title, water supply and execution risk. Water scarcity and land protests are reported by CNA, and no returns can be assumed.
