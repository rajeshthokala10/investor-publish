Blue Line trial trains edge toward Marathahalli

BMRCL aims to run trial trains from the Byappanahalli depot through KR Puram to Marathahalli by 15 November, Swarajya reports.

BMRCL aims to run trial trains on the Blue Line from the Byappanahalli depot via KR Puram to Marathahalli by 15 November, Swarajya reported on 10 November.

According to Swarajya, the 33kV cables and third rail were being progressively charged from 30 October between the depot and Marathahalli. Kinematic test runs are expected in November 2026.

Swarajya added that signalling integration remains the key hurdle before full testing can begin on the line.

The Bengaluru Live reported that the trial run is progressing from Byappanahalli towards KR Puram and Doddanekundi. The minister said the government is committed to completing pending works and opening the line at the earliest.

INVESTMENT COLUMN

Growth belts: promise versus progress

Sarjapur and Doddaballapur show how stage matters. The News Minute reports SWIFT City, 1,000 acres in Sarjapura, as announced with plug-and-play facilities planned. India Infra Hub lists KWIN City as announced, with acreage reported as 5,800 and 2,000, and NH 44 and 648 connectivity. Buyers may wish to check approvals.

Bidadi is listed as under construction, while Quantum City has land sanctioned. Those are more advanced stages, but buyers might still consider title clarity, water supply and execution risk before drawing conclusions.

The Bengaluru Business Corridor is tendered, per Swarajya, and Harohalli's second airport region is only proposed. Meanwhile the Blue Line is still in trials. Drivers at different stages deserve different weight; this is a consideration, not advice or a forecast.

Considerations, not investment advice. Check approvals, title and RERA registration before buying.
