TODAY’S TOP STORIES

Business Corridor land awards due by December, says chairperson

Bengaluru Business Corridor chairperson L K Atheeq says compensation will be disbursed by December 2026 as final notifications are issued at four cloverleaf junctions.

Bengaluru Business Corridor Ltd chairperson L K Atheeq said the government would complete preparation and approval of land acquisition awards and disburse compensation by December 2026, Deccan Herald reported on 1 November.

Final notifications for extra land at four cloverleaf junctions have been issued, according to the report. Awards will take one to two months to prepare, Deccan Herald said, citing the chairperson.

The corridor, also referred to as PRR-1, remains one of the outer city's most closely watched road projects, with land acquisition the step that determines how quickly work can proceed.

SKYDECK

BDA hires KPMG for Bengaluru's proposed skydeck tower

The Bengaluru Development Authority has hired KPMG as transaction adviser for the proposed 250-metre skydeck tower, estimated at Rs 600 crore, Deccan Herald reported on 2 November.

BDA has also finalised 46 acres in Kempegowda Layout for the project, according to the report. The Hindu reports that BDA has begun preparatory works.

Deccan Herald headlines the move as a financial plan for what it calls Bengaluru's tallest tower.

INVESTMENT COLUMN

Reading Bengaluru's outer growth areas

Outer-city growth stories rest on different drivers at different stages. Bidadi AI City is listed as under construction, according to The News Minute, while Quantum City at Hesaraghatta is described as approved, with land sanctioned.

SWIFT City, spread over 1,000 acres in Sarjapura, and KWIN City near Doddaballapura are both at the announced stage. KWIN is about 50 km from the city centre, with NH 44 and 648 connectivity reported.
