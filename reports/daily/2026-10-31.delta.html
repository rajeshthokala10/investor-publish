Pink Line elevated stretch clears safety check, awaits inauguration

The 7.5-km Kalena Agrahara–Tavarekere section has CMRS clearance; the state has asked the Centre for an inauguration date.

The 7.5 km elevated Pink Line stretch between Kalena Agrahara and Tavarekere received safety clearance from the Commissioner of Metro Railway Safety (Southern Circle) on Wednesday 28 October, Oneindia reported.

Greater Bengaluru Development Minister Krishna Byre Gowda said in a post on X that the stretch was 'ready for public service' and that the state had asked the Centre for an inauguration date. He indicated opening by mid-November.

Chief Minister DK Shivakumar said Prime Minister Narendra Modi would visit Bengaluru on 15 November and was likely to inaugurate the stretch; the date was still awaiting confirmation from the Centre. The Daily Jagran also reported Modi is expected to inaugurate it on 15 November.

BLUE LINE

Blue Line trials on KR Puram–Silk Board expected soon

Construction World reported on 28 October that trial runs on the 19.75-km KR Puram–Central Silk Board stretch were expected to begin by the end of October, with construction 87% complete and services targeted for mid-2027, subject to statutory clearances.

Minister Krishna Byre Gowda said station entry and exit works had reached 88 per cent completion. He said the project was already delayed by a year; officials blamed Cauvery water pipelines, drainage lines and traffic, and a court stay on tree felling was also linked to the delay.

INVESTMENT COLUMN

Reading Bengaluru's growth areas carefully

Metro is the clearest driver today: the Pink Line has safety clearance while the Blue Line is still under construction, with services targeted for mid-2027. Buyers near either corridor may want to check which stage the work has reached.

Outer-ring projects sit earlier on the path. SWIFT City and Quantum City are at announced stage, the Business Corridor is tendered, and the Bidadi AI City is approved. Buyers may wish to check approvals, title and water.

The second airport at Harohalli is only proposed, and KWIN City is announced. These are considerations, not predictions; execution risk remains.

Considerations, not investment advice. Check approvals, title and RERA registration before buying.
