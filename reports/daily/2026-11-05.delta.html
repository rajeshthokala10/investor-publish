INVESTMENT COLUMN

Reading Bengaluru's outer growth areas

Outer-ring projects sit at different stages. Bidadi AI City is listed as under construction, Quantum City in Hesaraghatta as approved, and SWIFT City in Sarjapur and KWIN City near Doddaballapura as announced.

Drivers vary: KWIN City is reported to have connectivity via NH 44 and 648, while the Bengaluru Business Corridor is at the tendered stage. The second airport region at Harohalli is only proposed.

Buyers may wish to consider approvals, title, water supply and execution risk, and to note the stage a project has reached rather than its announcement. The Karnataka township land notification is one such step. These are considerations, not advice.
