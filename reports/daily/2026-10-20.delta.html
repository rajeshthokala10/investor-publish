NGT halts tree felling for Hebbal Short Tunnel

The Green Tribunal's order freezes felling along a Bellary Road stretch until the case is disposed of, with the next hearing set for 15 December.

The National Green Tribunal on Friday 16 October halted all tree felling for the Hebbal Short Tunnel Project, Indian Opinion reported. The order covers a 2.2 km stretch of Bellary Road between Esteem Mall Junction and Veterinary College and stays until the matter is disposed of.

Vartha Bharati reported that the tribunal acted on an application by Aranya, a Mangaluru-based group. It added that the Advocate-General's assurance to the High Court had concerned Lalbagh, not Hebbal.

According to Vartha Bharati, the next hearing is on 15 December. Urban Acres also reports that the NGT has halted tree felling for the Hebbal tunnel project.

INVESTMENT COLUMN

Where Bengaluru's growth corridors actually stand

Bidadi is the clearest case of a driver at an advanced stage: The News Minute lists the township as approved, with GBDA as executing agency. BMRDA's integrated townships remain proposed, with Bidadi as pilot. Buyers might check approvals and land status before any launch.

In the north, Q-City at Hesaraghatta is approved, with land sanctioned. KWIN City is only announced, over 5,800 acres with a monorail planned. The Devanahalli aerospace plan was scrapped, a reminder that announcements can reverse. Plot buyers around Doddaballapur might verify title and water supply.

SWIFT City near Sarjapur is announced, across 1,000 acres with 150 ft roads planned. Harohalli is the leading contender for a second airport but remains proposed. Hosur and Attibele are being discussed around a proposed metro extension. These are earlier-stage drivers, so execution risk is higher.

Hebbal shows how a project can stall: the NGT halted tree felling for the tunnel. Metro-led pitches, such as Realty Today's note on north Bengaluru, are worth weighing against actual trial and opening dates. These are considerations, not advice or predictions.

Considerations, not investment advice. Check approvals, title and RERA registration before buying.
