INVESTMENT COLUMN

Reading Bengaluru's growth areas with care

Several outer areas are pitched on big projects. Q-City at Hesaraghatta has land sanctioned and is approved, while SWIFT City at Sarjapur is still in its acquisition phase, according to Land Conflict Watch. Stage matters.

Bidadi's AI City township is approved, with acreage under acquisition. KWIN City is announced, with a Tata Group pledge of ₹500 crore. Buyers may wish to check approvals, title and water for each.

Not every plan proceeds: acquisition for Devanahalli Aerospace Park land was dropped. Harohalli's second airport is only proposed. Execution risk is a consideration, not a prediction of returns, and this is not advice.

Considerations, not investment advice. Check approvals, title and RERA registration before buying.
