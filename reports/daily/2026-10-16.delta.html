INVESTMENT COLUMN

Growth areas: check which stage the promise has reached

Some outer-city projects have cleared formal steps. Bidadi Township is at the approved stage, with GBDA as executing agency, and Q-City has had land sanctioned at Hesaraghatta. Dr Shivaram Karanth Layout has High Court approval. Buyers may still want to confirm what approval covers.

Others remain announcements. SWIFT City is planned across 1,000 acres with 150 ft roads, and KWIN City across 5,800 acres with a monorail with stops every 800 metres. Both are at the announced stage, so execution risk is a consideration.

Plans also change. The Devanahalli aerospace park plan was scrapped, with fresh notices later reported for 439 acres, while Harohalli is described as the leading contender for a second airport at the proposed stage.

Today's news adds a caution on land governance: a sub-registrar was suspended over a Peripheral Ring Road land sale. Buyers may consider checking approvals, title, water supply and delivery track record. These are considerations, not advice.
