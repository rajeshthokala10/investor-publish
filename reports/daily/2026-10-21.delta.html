Hebbal Short Tunnel tree felling draws NGT halt and objections

The National Green Tribunal has stopped tree felling for the project, while the civic body fields objections from residents.

The NGT has halted tree felling for the Hebbal Short Tunnel Project in Bengaluru, reports The Hindu, in a setback for the north Bengaluru road project.

Separately, The Hindu reports that the Greater Bengaluru Authority has received over 1,500 objections to the proposed felling of trees for the tunnel.

Urban Acres says the project is putting Bengaluru's tree-clearing system to the test, a reference to how the city handles such approvals for large civic works.

INVESTMENT COLUMN

Reading Bengaluru's growth areas by stage

Growth areas sit at very different stages. Bidadi's township and Q-City at Hesaraghatta are reported as approved, while SWIFT City and KWIN City are only announced. Buyers may wish to weigh that gap.

Drivers vary: the Business Corridor has been awarded, while Harohalli's airport prospects remain a proposal. Devanahalli shows execution risk, where the aerospace park plan was scrapped.

Considerations include approvals, clear title, water supply and who executes the work. Tree-felling disputes at Hebbal show how civic projects can be delayed. None of this is advice.

Considerations, not investment advice. Check approvals, title and RERA registration before buying.
