Karnataka targets KWIN City plot allotments by March

The state aims to finish plot allotments in the planned knowledge city near Doddaballapur by March, reports say, alongside an investment push.

Karnataka aims to complete KWIN City plot allotments by March, The News Mill reports. India's News.Net reports the state also aims to attract 50,000 crore in investments to the project.

KWIN City, a knowledge city, was announced to spread over 5,800 acres, according to Outlook Business. It is located between Dobbaspete and Doddaballapura.

Swarajya reported that the plans include a central monorail with stops every 800 metres. The project remains at the announced stage, and the monorail is a plan, not a sanctioned work.

INVESTMENT COLUMN

Reading Bengaluru's growth areas: drivers and checks

Bidadi township, or AI City, is at the approved stage, with GBDA as executing agency, according to The News Minute. Q-City at Hesaraghatta has land allocated and sanctioned. Buyers may consider whether approval has become construction, and verify title and layout sanction.

SWIFT City near Sarjapur is only announced: The News Minute reports 1,000 acres near IT hubs, and The Realty Today reports planned 150 ft roads. Buyers may weigh that roads and jobs are plans, so execution risk and water supply deserve checking.

Harohalli is the leading contender for a second airport, but The South First lists it as proposed. Airport-led expectations are therefore early-stage considerations, not settled facts, and approvals should be confirmed before any commitment.

Considerations, not investment advice. Check approvals, title and RERA registration before buying.
