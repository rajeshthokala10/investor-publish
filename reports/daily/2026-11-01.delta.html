K-RIDE awards Kanaka Line bridge contracts worth ₹105.66 crore

K-RIDE issues acceptance letters for four road overbridges and one underbridge on Corridor-4 of Bengaluru's suburban rail network.

K-RIDE/Bi-RIDE issued letters of acceptance on 30 October 2026 to ESSVY-SLNS (JV) for two packages worth about ₹105.66 crore (excluding GST), according to The Bengaluru Live. The works cover four road overbridges and one road underbridge on the Heelalige–Rajanukunte Kanaka Line (Corridor-4).

Package-1, at ₹54.83 crore excluding GST, covers two ROBs and one RUB. Package-2, at ₹50.83 crore, covers two ROBs. Both include service roads and drainage, the report said.

Each package carries an 18-month completion period. Physical construction is expected to begin shortly, per the report.

CIVIC

GBA e-Khata camps dispose of 618 applications

GBA's Saturday 'Nanna e-Khata, Nanna Hakku' camps on 31 October disposed of 618 applications at 62 Bengaluru centres, according to The Bengaluru Live.

The disposals included 233 e-Khata, 99 mutation, 251 correction, 27 B-to-A khata and 8 new khata cases. The camps received 961 applications and 1,244 citizens visited.
