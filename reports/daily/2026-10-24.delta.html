Pink Line stretch waits on Centre's nod to open

Karnataka says trial runs on the 7.5-km Kalena Agrahara–Tavarekere elevated section are complete; the state is now waiting for Union government approval.

Minister Krishna Byre Gowda said on 22 October that Karnataka is awaiting Union government approval to open the 7.5-km Kalena Agrahara–Tavarekere stretch of the Pink Line, according to Asianet Newsable. He said all trial runs are complete.

BMRCL expects final operational clearance within four to five days, the minister said. No opening date has been confirmed.

Chief Minister D.K. Shivakumar has written to the Centre seeking an inauguration date and had not received a reply as of 23 October. The state plans to meet the Union Minister the following week, Asianet Newsable reported.

The Hindu also reports that approval is expected within five days.

HEBBAL

NGT bars tree felling for Hebbal Short Tunnel

The National Green Tribunal has restrained the Bangalore Development Authority from felling any trees for the Hebbal Short Tunnel until the next hearing on 15 December, The News Minute reported on 21 October.

The Greater Bengaluru Authority received more than 1,500 objections to the proposed felling of 1,128 trees. The objections will go to the tree expert committee and the BDA, which is implementing the project.

The Indian Express calls the order a win for green activists, while The Hindu reports around 1,600 objections. Urban Acres says the project faces NGT, traffic and cost tests.

Blue Line

Blue Line trial run date remains unsettled

BMRCL has been told to begin trial runs on the 19.75-km KR Puram–Central Silk Board stretch by end-October 2026 and aims to open it by July 2027. Deccan Herald reported the minister expects a KR Puram–Marathahalli trial by October-end, while BMRCL's MD on 19 October targeted 15 November.

Blue Line

Minister blames court stay and utility shifts for delays

Krishna Byre Gowda attributed Blue Line delays to a roughly year-long court stay on tree felling near KR Puram, 6–8 months lost relocating Cauvery water and drainage lines, and night-only work on the Outer Ring Road. He called the situation unacceptable.

Blue Line

Unneeded ORR Metro barricades ordered removed

Minister Krishna Byre Gowda on 22 October ordered officials to remove Metro barricades on the Outer Ring Road where construction is complete, saying barricades retained without need were worsening congestion.

INVESTMENT COLUMN

Reading Bengaluru's growth areas by stage

Several outer growth areas have moved beyond announcement. Q-City at Hesaraghatta is approved, with land allocated and sanctioned. Bidadi Township/AI City is approved, with GBDA as executing agency. Buyers may wish to check how far approvals have turned into works.

Others remain at an earlier stage. SWIFT City near Sarjapur is announced, with 150 ft roads planned. KWIN City is announced, with a central monorail planned. The Harohalli second airport region is only proposed. Considerations include land title, water and execution risk.

Plans can change: Karnataka dropped acquisition of over 1,777 acres in 13 villages for the Devanahalli Aerospace Park, and its stage is unclear. BMRDA's three proposed townships, with Bidadi as pilot, are also at proposal stage.

Today's news shows how timelines slip. The STRR Hoskote–Hosur stretch awaits an overbridge, and NHAI is looking at December for a phased opening. These are considerations, not advice or predicted returns.

Considerations, not investment advice. Check approvals, title and RERA registration before buying.
