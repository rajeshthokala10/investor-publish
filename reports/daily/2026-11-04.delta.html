Pink Line inauguration date firms up, with reports differing

Namma Metro's elevated Pink Line is reported to have safety clearance, with the Prime Minister expected to open it in mid-November.

Deccan Herald reports that Prime Minister Narendra Modi is to inaugurate Namma Metro's elevated Pink Line on November 13. Urban Acres also reports the launch is set for November 13.

Other outlets give a different date. Construction World says Mr Modi may inaugurate the line on November 15, while Urban Acres separately reports the line will open to the public on November 15.

Reports differ on whether November 15 is the inauguration or the public opening. No single official schedule is cited in today's reports, so commuters should wait for a formal announcement.

INVESTMENT COLUMN

Reading Bengaluru's outer growth areas

Several outer areas are driven by planned cities rather than finished infrastructure. SWIFT City in Sarjapur is announced, spread over 1,000 acres. KWIN City near Doddaballapura is also only announced. Buyers should treat both as plans.

Further along, Quantum City at Hesaraghatta is described as approved, with land sanctioned, and Bidadi AI City is listed as under construction. Even so, a buyer should check approvals, title and water before relying on stage labels.

Connectivity matters too: the Bengaluru Business Corridor is at tender stage and the Harohalli second airport region is only proposed. This week's news on the township land notification and PRR-1 shows execution is staged. These are considerations, not advice.

Considerations, not investment advice. Check approvals, title and RERA registration before buying.
