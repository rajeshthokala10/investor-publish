About us

Independent research on Bengaluru real estate

Bengaluru Property Review tracks the city’s housing market and the infrastructure that shapes it. Every morning our research desk reviews government agencies, Namma Metro (BMRCL), K-RIDE, Karnataka RERA, research firms, developers and established newspapers, and publishes what changed.

Figures are always attributed to the organisation that published them. Plans are reported at the stage they have actually reached — proposed, approved, under construction or operational — so readers can tell announcements from delivery.
