Bangalore Realestate Delta — Problem statement & USP

The problem

Buying or investing in Bengaluru property means deciding on fragmented, noisy and often one-sided information.

  • Scattered: news about the metro, roads, layouts and approvals is spread across BMRCL, K-RIDE, BDA, GBA, BMRDA, K-RERA, court orders, dozens of newspapers and property portals. Nobody shows what changed today.
  • Hype-led: broker reels, sponsored "realty news" and developer releases promise "guaranteed appreciation". Their claims are repeated until they look like facts.
  • Announcements read as delivery: a proposal, an MoU, a cabinet approval and an operational metro line are reported as if they were the same thing. Buyers pay today for infrastructure that may be years away.
  • Numbers don't compare: land rates are quoted next to super-built-up apartment rates, and one research firm's figure is stitched onto another's.
  • No time to track it: quarterly reports are slow and paywalled, and busy professionals and NRIs can't follow every corridor every day.

What we solve

One trusted place that tells you what changed in Bengaluru real estate and infrastructure. It is refreshed every morning and evening, every item carries its source, and checking rules separate facts from hype.

For Outcome
Home buyers & NRIs Know which metro lines, roads and projects actually moved, by area, before deciding where to buy
Investors Track growth corridors and towns (Hoskote, Devanahalli, Sarjapur, Kanakapura, Bidadi…) by stage reached, not headlines
Brokers, advisors, banks Shareable, sourced daily briefing; consistent area price levels; builder activity
Developers Daily competitive and infrastructure watch across every zone

USP — why this is different

  1. Evidence-first:
    • Every figure is attributed and checked against the page it came from.
    • Two papers quoting the same research report count as one source.
    • Developers, brokers, portals and influencers are kept separate from independent reporting.
    • "Guaranteed returns" language is filtered out.
  2. Announcement vs delivery: projects are tracked on an 8-stage ladder (proposed → announced → approved → funded → tendered → awarded → under construction → operational). A stage is shown only when the source text supports it.
  3. Metro-first lens:
    • A live network view of every Namma Metro line, with stations, interchanges and moving trains.
    • A line-by-line status board.
    • A daily Metro Watch.
  4. Three products, one engine:
    • Bangalore Realestate Delta, a 5-page daily magazine at 6:00 IST: metro, development, government and news from every area. No statistics noise.
    • An 18:00 evening refresh.
    • Bengaluru Property Review, a 10-page monthly magazine with corridor features, growth towns, and plots vs villas vs apartments.
  5. Comparable numbers:
    • Prices are normalised to ₹/sq ft on the same basis; land rates are never ranked against apartment rates.
    • Each trend chart uses one research firm's data.
  6. The writers can't invent figures: the AI writers have no internet access and use only checked facts. Any sentence with a number not found in its source is removed automatically before publishing.
  7. Automated and low-cost: the research desk runs unattended twice a day for about $3–4. It has budgets, retries, audit logs, and a clear "no news" vs "research failed" distinction.
